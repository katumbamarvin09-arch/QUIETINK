# 09 — Letters to Future Me Guide

The **Letters to Future Me** feature allows you to send an intimate letter across time.

---

## The Envelope Aesthetic

Unlike standard journal entries, a letter to your future self has ceremony:
- Formal salutation: *"Dear future me,"*
- Styled in an elegant stationery envelope layout.
- Tagged with a future opening date.

---

## Writing Suggestions for Future Me

- **Where you are right now**: What does your desk look like? What tea are you drinking? What song is stuck in your head?
- **Current worries**: What is keeping you up at night? (Looking back 1 year later often reveals that what felt catastrophic resolved peacefully).
- **Questions for yourself**: Did you finish that book? Are you still in touch with old friends? Did you take that trip?
- **Reminders of love**: Remind yourself of the values and kindnesses that matter most to you.
