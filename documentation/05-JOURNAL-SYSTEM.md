# 05 — The Journal System & Writing Room

The Writing Room is the sanctuary heart of QuietInk. It is designed to feel like opening an unlined, high-quality private paper journal.

---

## Key Writing Room Features

1. **Distraction-Free Solitude Mode**:
   - Click **Solitude Mode** (or the expand icon) in the header.
   - All navigation bars, footers, and browser distractions fade away.
   - The writing surface expands to the center of the screen with generous negative space.
2. **Autosave Engine**:
   - Every stroke of your keyboard is debounced and saved automatically every 2 seconds (`siteConfig.preferences.autosaveIntervalMs`).
   - A quiet checkmark ("Saved to your quiet space") appears without obtrusive popups.
3. **Typography & Font Sizing**:
   - Controls for `A-` and `A+` let you adjust font size between 14px and 28px.
   - Toggle between literary Serif (`Lora`, `Cormorant Garamond`) and modern Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
4. **Writing Modes**:
   - **Free Journal**: Stream of consciousness without rules.
   - **Untangle Thoughts**: Step-by-step clarity for anxious or crowded thoughts.
   - **Gratitude**: Noticing small, specific gifts of the day.
   - **Reflection**: Looking back on past milestones or decisions.
   - **Gentle Goals**: Aspirations without pressure or urgency.
   - **Creative Ideas**: Sparks, concepts, and storytelling.
   - **Letter to Self**: Honest dialogue with your inner self.
   - **Daily Rhythm**: Morning, afternoon, and evening summary.
5. **Emotional Weather**:
   - Attach a gentle mood tag: ☀️ Bright, 🌤️ Calm, 🌥️ Cloudy, 🌧️ Heavy, 🌙 Quiet, 🌈 Hopeful.
   - Filter your past journals in the Dashboard or Library by emotional weather.
