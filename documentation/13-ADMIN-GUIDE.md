# 13 — Administrator Guide & Privacy Covenant

QuietInk includes an administrator interface designed around ethical stewardship.

---

## Accessing the Admin Panel

In the footer or via the navigation menu, click the **Admin** button to open the Platform Administration modal.

Sections available:
1. **System Overview**: Inspect platform name, active storage mode, and audio synthesis engine.
2. **Prompt Library**: Review the rotating list of daily quiet questions, and add new prompts directly.
3. **Atmospheres**: Inspect the 8 Calm Mode environments, quotes, and canvas effect parameters.
4. **Privacy Integrity**: Audit the platform's zero-surveillance guarantees.

---

## The Zero-Surveillance Rule

> **CRITICAL RULE**: Administrators MUST NOT have casual backdoor access to read user journal entries.

In conventional social apps, administrators frequently have access to user feeds.
In QuietInk:
- Personal journals are private sanctuaries.
- The administrator's role is to maintain uptime, curate soothing prompts, and ensure system reliability—never to browse private writings.
- If content moderation is ever legally mandated, it must be clearly stated in the Privacy Policy and restricted to publicly published or shared content.
